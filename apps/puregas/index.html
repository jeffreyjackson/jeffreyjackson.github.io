Pure Gas Support

Pure Gas helps you find ethanol-free gasoline stations, plan trips around them, and track your fuel economy.

Contact

For questions, bug reports or feedback about the app, email jeffreyclarkejackson@gmail.com. Please include your iOS version and the app version (shown in Settings in the app).

Station information

Station listings come from the community-maintained database at pure-gas.org. If a station has closed or no longer sells ethanol-free fuel, use Report an Issue on the station's page in the app, which opens pure-gas.org so the listing can be corrected for everyone.

Purchases

PureGas Pro and tips are processed by Apple. To restore PureGas Pro on a new device, use Restore Purchases in the app's Settings. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

Pure Gas collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
